Key Functionalities

Supports multiple barcode formats: EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128, Code 39, GS1-128, ISBN and ISSN.

Generates structured barcodes using company/category rules and a unique sequence

Works with both Product Templates and Product Variants

One-click overwrite of existing barcodes

Barcode images shown directly in product form views

Base64 PNG images usable in PDF Reports or custom labels

Font scaling & readable text under barcodes

Error handling, logging, and permission control

In the Product view, click the Action button and select the "Product Barcoded Generator" option from the list.

Provide the necessary inputs in the wizard based on your needs.

Fill in the required fields and click Product barcode generator to generate barcode instantly.

To create missing barcode images -> In the Product view, click the Action button and select the "Barcode Missing Image Generator".

How to Use

Ensure Access:  Add user to the group "Product Barcode Generator User".

Go to:  Inventory > Products > Products or Product Variants.

Select Products:  Use checkboxes to select one or more records.

Click Action:  Choose "Generate Barcode" from the Action menu.

Wizard:  Choose barcode type, and whether to overwrite existing values.

Apply:  Click “Apply” to generate and save the barcode images.
